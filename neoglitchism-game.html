    Neoglitchism

        about neoglitchism

        Concrete art asked viewers to stop looking for stories and look at form: color, line and plane as facts in their own right. Neo-concretism answered by handing the work to the body and turning spectators into participants. Neoglitchism hands it to the player. Every piece in this world arrives corrupted, and approaching it, choosing to look, is what restores the signal.

        Palettes and measurements in each piece's signal analysis are computed in your browser from the image's own pixels. Progress is saved on this device.
